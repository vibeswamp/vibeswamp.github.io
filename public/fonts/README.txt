Fonts of vibeswamp.github.io, served from the site itself instead of Google Fonts (12.11.2026), so visitors' addresses
do not go to a third party and the page needs one server less.

Bitter, Overpass and Tektur, from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com), files as Google serves them (woff2, split by
alphabet: latin, latin-ext, cyrillic, cyrillic-ext; variable weight). All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The copyright notices are inside each font file.

The @font-face rules are at the top of src/style.css.
