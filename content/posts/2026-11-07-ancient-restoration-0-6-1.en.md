Versions 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 of Ancient Restoration came out on 6 and 7 November 2026.

- Ancient Obsession is now built into the mod, and it can be switched off. Now and then a skilled colonist becomes obsessed with an ancient wreck, demands parts and restores it alone into a named legend.
- It only happens in colonies with three or more grown-up colonists.
- The standalone [Ancient Obsession](/projects/ancient-obsession/) stays on the Workshop for people who play without Ancient Restoration.
- The Workshop page is reorganised and has a short line of facts for modpack builders.

![A legendary mini-turret after an obsession, with its own name and story.](assets/ancient-obsession/legend-ingame.jpg)

What came in 0.5.0, from greyed-out Restore buttons that say why to the gene bank from Alpha Genes, is in [the post about 0.5.0](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-5-0/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
