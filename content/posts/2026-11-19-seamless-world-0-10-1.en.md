Version 0.10.1 of Seamless World came out on 19 November 2026. It is still a beta, for RimWorld 1.7 and 1.6.

- Fixed: raiders who chase a colonist over the map edge now follow into the neighbor zone. Before, the chase broke at the edge and they simply walked off the map.
- Checked in the game on a raid at the map edge.

What came last time is in [the post about 0.10.0](/blog/seamless-world-0-10-0/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
