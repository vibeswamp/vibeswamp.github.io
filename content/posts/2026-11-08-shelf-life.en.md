On 8 November 2026 a new mod for RimWorld 1.7 came out on the Steam Workshop: [Shelf Life](/projects/shelf-life/). It is for big modpacks, where storage takes half the base and the camera starts to stutter over it.

- A shelf is upgraded where it stands, without taking it apart or moving the goods. Five levels: 3, 6, 10, 18 and 30 stacks per cell. Level 1 is the game's own shelf; after that each level holds about 1.5 to 2 times the one before, and costs more: steel, components, plasteel and Construction up to 10.
- The levels look like the game: it is the same RimWorld shelf with boards and steel added to it.
- A tarp for any shelf: the goods under it are not drawn, only one icon of what is inside. There are closed crates for weapons and armour, and boxes for small things.
- It is made for the speed of big storage: nothing runs every tick, and a full store on 5 level-5 shelves is searched as fast as one on 50 ordinary shelves. Goods under a tarp cost the game nothing until someone touches them.
- Adaptive Storage racks such as Warehouse Storage get levels too. The mod is tested in game with Pick Up And Haul, Common Sense, Adaptive Storage, Warehouse Storage, Storage Refill Hysteresis and Performance Optimizer.
- English, Russian and Ukrainian. Needs Harmony.
