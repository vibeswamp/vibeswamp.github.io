Version 0.5.0 of Ancient Restoration came out on 6 November 2026. Most of it comes from your comments on the Workshop.

- Fixed: the VQE Ancients chemfuel generator refused to restore. Thanks to sidorovich.
- The huge ancient gene bank from Alpha Genes becomes one big working gene bank that holds 16 genepacks. The faint lab lamps and worn shelves from Alpha Genes and the empty cryptosleep caskets from VQE Ancients can be restored too. Thanks to Guk5.
- Sealed crates, safes, boxes and pallets from the base game and Odyssey become storage once they are opened and empty.
- An ancient object the mod cannot restore now shows a greyed-out Restore button that says why: which mod it needs, that it already works, that it belongs to a quest, or that nothing with the same purpose can be made of it. Thanks to Guk5 and Starman.
- Over a hundred such leftovers can be taken apart for parts, including the ruined lab machines from VQE Ancients. Thanks to Starman.
- A restored ancient barrel can be moved like a small shelf. Thanks to \*HARDBASS INTENSIFIED\* for asking.
