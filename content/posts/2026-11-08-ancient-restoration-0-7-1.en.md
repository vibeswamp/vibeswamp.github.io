On 8 November 2026 two more updates came out: Ancient Restoration 0.7.1 and the standalone Ancient Obsession 0.6.2.

**Ancient Restoration 0.7.1** comes from players' comments on the Workshop.

- Ancient nav beacons can now be repaired into ship landing beacons. This needs the Royalty DLC. A repaired beacon keeps its rusty look but already works: together with ordinary beacons it marks out a landing pad. Later you can upgrade it to the standard beacon.
- The Restore button's tooltip is split into blocks instead of one wall of text: what it needs, what comes next, and notes about damage and refunds.

![Select a wreck and order a quick or a proper repair.](assets/ancient-restoration/15-i-gizmos.jpg)

**Ancient Obsession 0.6.2** is the [standalone mod](/projects/ancient-obsession/) for people who play without Ancient Restoration. It now has the same obsession code as the module built into Ancient Restoration, with the same fix: legendary restorations do not break down. Nothing else changes in play.

![A legend's card: a mini-turret named by its master, which does not break down.](assets/ancient-obsession/03-legend-card.jpg)

What came last time is in [the post about Ancient Restoration 0.6.2](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-6-2/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the updates by itself.
