Ancient Obsession 0.6.0 came out on 7 November 2026.

- The mod is built from the same code as the obsession module inside [Ancient Restoration](/projects/ancient-restoration/) 0.6, so from now on both get the same updates.
- If both mods are on, Ancient Obsession runs and Ancient Restoration keeps its own copy off. You only need the standalone mod if you play without Ancient Restoration.
- An obsession needs at least 3 free adult colonists, with the full chance from 5. The threshold is in the settings.
- 10 languages, Ukrainian now among them.

![A colonist on their knees before a heap of ancient junk.](assets/ancient-obsession/scene.png)

Saves are compatible: obsessions in progress and finished legends stay. This update reset the mod settings to their defaults, so if you had changed them, take another look.
