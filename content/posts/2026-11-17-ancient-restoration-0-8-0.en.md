Version 0.8.0 of Ancient Restoration came out on 17 November 2026. Most of it comes from players' comments on the Workshop.

- RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 in one Workshop item: the game picks the right build by itself.
- Vault props from Vanilla Furniture Expanded – Props and Decor can be repaired. The ancient cooler cools again, the broken defensive turret becomes a working mini-turret, and the water filter becomes a water treatment plant from Dubs Bad Hygiene (that mod is needed for it). Thanks to Renix.
- The same mod has 23 copies of vault furniture from Vanilla Quests Expanded – Ancients. They now restore the same way as the originals. Thanks to bumrum for the question about compatibility.
- With the Odyssey DLC: the ancient drilling platform becomes a drill rig, and a fallen ancient wind turbine stands up and works again.
- 204 restoration chains in total. Tested in the game on 1.7 and on 1.6.

The error with modded materials from the bug thread ("invalid explicit stuff") is not fixed in this version yet. It will come in a separate fix.

What came last time is in [the post about 0.7.2](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-7-2/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
