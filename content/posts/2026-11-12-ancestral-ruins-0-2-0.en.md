Version 0.2.0 of Ancestral Ruins came out on 12 November 2026. It is still a beta: your past colonies come back as ruins in your next worlds.

- Your fallen colonists now lie in the ruins where they fell, with their weapons. Any resurrection brings them back into your colony: serum, psycast, ritual or another mod.
- A Ukrainian translation. The mod is now in Russian, Ukrainian and English.
- Its own icon in the mod list and a new cover in the style of our other mods.
- Saves are compatible, and the mod can be added to a running game.

![A ruin of an old colony: a fallen colonist lies where they fell, with graves nearby.](assets/ancestral-ruins/fallen-020.jpg)

There are now links with our other mods. Each works only if that mod is installed, and all were checked in game together:

- [Trodden Trails](/projects/trodden-trails/): the old colony's footpaths move into the ruin, faded with age.
- [Ancient Restoration](/projects/ancient-restoration/) and [Ancient Obsession](/projects/ancient-obsession/): the old colony's legend lies in the ruin as a wreck. Restore it and it gets its old name back.
- [Ancient Strata](/projects/ancient-strata/): dig under the ruin and you may find the old colony's cellar.
- [Told Twice](/projects/told-twice/): finding your old colonists is written into the colony's chronicle.
- [Challenges & Wagers](/projects/challenges-wagers/): a secret trophy. A hint: try to find your own grave.

![The mod's cover: "Here lies Bob. Again."](assets/ancestral-ruins/cover-020.png)

If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
