On 17 November 2026 three more mods got updates. All three now run on RimWorld 1.6 as well as 1.7: one Workshop item, and the game picks the right build by itself. Each one was tested in the game on both versions.

**[Ancestral Ruins](/projects/ancestral-ruins/) 0.6.0**

- A new setting, an idea from the player Jack Maker: a ruin appears at the same latitude and longitude where the colony stood, whatever the seed of the new world.
- If that spot is taken or under water, the ruin moves to the nearest land (up to 15 tiles away) or is skipped, as you choose.
- This works for colonies recorded from 0.6.0 on. Older records are placed by terrain type, as before.
- Colony records are shared between 1.6 and 1.7.

**[Told Twice](/projects/told-twice/) 0.3.0** supports RimWorld 1.6. The content is the same.

**[Shelf Life](/projects/shelf-life/) 0.6.0** supports RimWorld 1.6, including the bridges to Adaptive Storage and Pick Up And Haul.

Earlier the same day [Ancient Restoration 0.8.0](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-8-0/) came out on 1.6 too. If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the updates by itself.
