Trodden Trails, a new mod for RimWorld 1.7, appeared on the Steam Workshop on 6 November 2026. It needs Harmony.

Caravans that keep using one route wear a trail into the world map, and the next trip is faster: about 10% after three trips, about 20% after seven. A trail nobody uses grows back over a few weeks. It is always weaker than a road and never appears where a road already is.

- Raiders, traders and visitors wear the way they come by, so the map slowly shows where they come from.
- In the colony, footpaths form where people walk often. In the rain bare soil turns to mud, and busy rough stone wears smooth.
- Everything is drawn over the ground, so the mod can be added to a running save and removed.
