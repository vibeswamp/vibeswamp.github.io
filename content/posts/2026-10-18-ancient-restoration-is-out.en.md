Ancient Restoration appeared on the Steam Workshop on 18 October 2026. It is a mod for RimWorld 1.7.

It is about the ancient wrecks you find in ruins. Instead of scrapping a rusted turret for steel, you can repair it where it stands: first back to working ancient gear, later to its modern counterpart.

![An ancient turret in five stages: wreck, rusty, refurbished, standard and modern.](assets/ancient-restoration/07-keyart.jpg)

The mod is free. It is [on the Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3822574440), and [the mod page](/projects/ancient-restoration/) here has all the pictures.
