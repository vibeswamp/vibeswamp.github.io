Version 0.3.2 of Trodden Trails came out on 12 November 2026. It is a fix for those who play together with [Ancestral Ruins](/projects/ancestral-ruins/).

Ancestral Ruins brings an old colony back as a ruin in a new world, and the trails worn in it move there too. If the map with the ruin was still being generated, the trails did not show up on it. Now they do: this was checked by running both mods together.

What the mod does is in [the post about its release](/blog/trodden-trails-is-out/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
