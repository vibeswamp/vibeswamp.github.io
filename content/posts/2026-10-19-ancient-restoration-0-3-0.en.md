Version 0.3.0 of Ancient Restoration came out on 19 October 2026, the day after the mod first appeared on the Workshop.

- Every stage of a repair now looks different, so a quick patch-up and a proper repair are easy to tell apart.
- 146 options instead of 121.
- Eight languages.

![Base game: an ancient bed, generator and electric stove as a wreck, restored and modernized.](assets/ancient-restoration/13-i-vanilla.jpg)

If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
