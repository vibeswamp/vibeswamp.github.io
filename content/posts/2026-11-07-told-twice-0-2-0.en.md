Told Twice 0.2.0 came out on 7 November 2026.

- Ukrainian, next to English and Russian.
- The chronicle notices remains found with [Ancestral Ruins](/projects/ancestral-ruins/) and vaults opened with Ancient Strata. Ancient Strata is not out yet.
- Gender and number agreement in the chronicle texts is fixed.

![The raiders run, and their leaflet already calls it a heroic victory.](assets/told-twice/victory.jpg)

A reminder of what the mod does. It keeps your colony's annals as you play, and the enemy who hurt you most tells the same years its own way, with names misheard and numbers in its favour. Raid leaders may bring their side's leaflet, and you can save the chronicle as a web page or as Markdown for Reddit. The text comes from hand-written templates, with no AI and no internet. Old saves work. [More about the mod](/projects/told-twice/).
