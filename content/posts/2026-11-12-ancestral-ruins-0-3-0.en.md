Version 0.3.0 of Ancestral Ruins came out on 12 November 2026, the same day as [0.2.0](/blog/ancestral-ruins-0-2-0/). It is still a beta.

Until now only abandoned colonies and those where everyone died came back as ruins. Now a colony you simply left in its save to start a new game comes back too.

- When you save the game, the mod records every colony older than 15 days and refreshes the record at most once every 15 game days.
- One record per colony. When the colony is abandoned or everyone in it dies, the final record goes to the same place.
- A colony never appears as ruins in the world where it still lives.
- A new setting, "Record living colonies when saving the game", is on by default.
- Saves are compatible. Load and save a colony from an old save once, and it will reach your next worlds.

![On the world map: the ruins of the colony and how long ago it lived.](assets/ancestral-ruins/world-map.jpg)

If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
