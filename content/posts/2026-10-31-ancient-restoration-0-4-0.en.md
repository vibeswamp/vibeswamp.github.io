Version 0.4.0 of Ancient Restoration came out on 31 October 2026.

- A restored building keeps the model of its wreck, only repaired. Generators, crates, antennas, IV stands and shelves keep their picture, ancient beds stay white, ancient toilets stay ancient.
- Restored machines get a thin dark cable on steel clamps instead of the fat orange tube.
- The restore button now says how many research projects a new building would still need and how much cheaper the repair is.
- 9 more crates and containers can be restored, from the base game, Odyssey, Ancient Urban Ruins and Deadlife. Thanks to Guk5.
- Ancient quirks, an upside with a catch: a restored generator gives 25% more power but sometimes stalls, a bed is hard but helps immunity, lamps light wider. They can be turned off in the mod settings.
- A repaired device keeps its purpose: the IV stand stays an IV stand, the hospital armchair stays a hospital seat.
- Fixed: enemy sieges could stall because of restored mortars. Thanks to 一念之差 for the report.
