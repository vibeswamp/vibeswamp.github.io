[KFSOS Lite](/projects/kfsos-lite/), a survival modpack for Minecraft 1.20.1 on Forge, is in pre-alpha and about 40% done.

| What | How many |
|---|---|
| Mods | 295, or 316 with bundled libraries |
| Our own mods | 14 |
| Quest chapters | 80, 38 of them visible, with 692 tasks on the main line |
| Guide entries | 339, in Russian and English |
| TACZ weapon packs | 18 |
| Loading-screen tracks | 15 |

The authored starting district, lighter hordes, searching bodies, hit sounds, and the video and music on the loading screen already work. The latest update fixed recipe links in the guide and removed needless warnings from the game log.

Left before an open test:

- play story chapters 1 to 3 through in the real game;
- fit the textures so the pack also runs on older graphics cards;
- move the fixes for other authors' mods into our own code;
- sort out repeats in the weapon packs, check licences and fix the dark zombie silhouettes in the starting district.

No release date yet.
