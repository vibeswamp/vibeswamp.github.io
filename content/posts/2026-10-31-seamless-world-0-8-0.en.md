Version 0.8.0 of Seamless / Borderless World came out on 31 October 2026. It is still a beta.

- Select colonists or vehicles and right-click beyond the edge: they cross together and walk on to that very spot.
- Before you click, a line shows the way and the rough time.
- Number pad keys 7, 9, 4, 6, 1 and 3 jump to a neighbour zone, 5 goes home. The keys can be changed in the controls.
- Keep pushing the camera against the edge, and it carries on into the neighbour.
- Fixes on the border: vehicles are not sent running for the edge, chasers aim for any free edge cell, Anomaly entities and bosses stay put.
- Works with Ancient Restoration: a zone with a wreck under repair is no longer forgotten while you are away.

![A vehicle crossing into the neighbouring zone, with the Vehicle Framework mod.](assets/seamless-world/s05-vehicle.jpg)

Next in the plans is fog over the neighbours: you will see only what your people or a watchtower can see. Ideas for what comes after are collected in the Discussions on [the Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823099600).
