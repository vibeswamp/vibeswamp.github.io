Challenges & Wagers 0.1.1 came out on 7 November 2026.

- Ukrainian translation of the whole interface and of every wager and achievement.
- The English run card lines read naturally now ("No trading, won" instead of "No trading: Won").

A reminder of what the mod does. Before the first day is over, you wager on rules for the colony, like no trading or nobody dies, and keep them for a year. Every trophy carries a seal: one save or reloads, how many mods. Nothing is forbidden, everything is written down. [More about the mod](/projects/challenges-wagers/).
