Besides the two RimWorld mods, four more projects are on the workbench. Here is where each one stands on 6 November 2026. These are plans, not promises.

## Scout mods for PEAK

Three mods with one shared scout passport: an item journal, gear on a paper doll from the game's own cosmetics, and badges that start as rumours. The packages for Thunderstore are built. Before release I want to play it with friends in a real co-op lobby. [More about the mods](/projects/peak-scouts/).

## KFSOS Lights and Wearables

Two mods from my Minecraft modpack: rechargeable lights for the hand, the head and the gun, and worn things that give small effects. Both build and pass their tests. Next comes a check in the game, then Modrinth. [More about the mods](/projects/kfsos-minecraft/).

## Toi-Late

My own game in Godot: a night shift where the toilet gives things back. The first three days are playable, in Russian for now. Next I play it through myself, then a closed playtest on itch.io. [More, with screenshots](/projects/toi-late/).

## SCRAPWELD: Vermin to Apex

My own game in Unreal Engine 5: a cyborg tears parts out of enemies and bolts them onto itself. Act I is playable on Windows, and next is the whole act up to the boss Varg. [More, with screenshots](/projects/scrapweld/).
