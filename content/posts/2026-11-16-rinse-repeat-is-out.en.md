[Rinse & Repeat](/projects/rinse-repeat/) came out on the Steam Workshop on 16 November 2026, version 0.5.2. It runs on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7.

- Colonists carry clothes taken from a corpse to any water, a river, a lake, a marsh or a hot spring, and wash the mark off: the colour and the price come back.
- Water alone is slow and wears the cloth. Soap is twice as fast. Soap is boiled from tallow and ash on a campfire or a stove: plain, scented, and a pink one colonists had better not ask about.
- A Laundry job and a habit: in their free time colonists wash what they wear. Clean clothes lift the mood.
- A washboard with a tub lets you wash indoors. The tub is filled with buckets, by rain or from Dubs Bad Hygiene plumbing.
- A new mental break, a laundry frenzy: the colonist washes everything in sight.
- Soap bubbles rise over whoever is washing, so you see the launderer at a glance.

![Before and after washing: the corpse mark is gone, and clean clothes replace the mood penalty.](assets/rinse-repeat/04-tainted-before-after.jpg)

The mod works next to Dubs Bad Hygiene and Simple Washing Machine and can be added to a running game. Questions and bugs go to our Discord, where the mod has its own channel.
