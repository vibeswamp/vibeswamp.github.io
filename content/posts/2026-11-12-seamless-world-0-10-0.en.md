Version 0.10.0 of Seamless World came out on 12 November 2026. It is still a beta. The main change: the mod now runs on RimWorld 1.6 too.

- One Workshop item for both versions of the game. The mod has its own build for 1.6 and for 1.7, and the game picks the right one.
- Everything from 0.9 works on 1.6: neighbor zones, crossing the edge, caravans, the fog and the "Prepare to remove the mod" button.
- [RimVision](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815721736) is only out for 1.6 so far, and there it works together with Seamless World: both the 3D overview and first person.
- Checked in game on 1.6 and 1.7: six neighbor zones, a caravan, a round trip across the edge and a clean removal of the mod, with no errors.

![The new mod preview, a meme: "One does not simply walk to the neighbors", with a map and a path across the zone border.](assets/seamless-world/meme-sheet-en.jpg)

On 1.7 the mod works the same as in 0.9.0. What came last time is in [the post about 0.9.0](/blog/seamless-world-0-9-0/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
