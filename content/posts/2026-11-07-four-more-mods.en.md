Between 31 October and 6 November four more of my mods for RimWorld 1.7 went up on the Steam Workshop, and now all of them are open to everyone.

## Challenges & Wagers

Bet the storyteller on rules for your colony, such as no guns or nobody dies, keep them for a year and win a trophy. [More about the mod](/projects/challenges-wagers/).

## Told Twice

The colony's annals as you play, and next to them the enemy's version of the same years, with the names wrong and the numbers in their favour. [More about the mod](/projects/told-twice/).

![One raid, told twice: your chronicle and the raiders' version.](assets/told-twice/two-voices.jpg)

## Ancestral Ruins

Your old colonies come back as ruins in your next worlds, with their walls, graves and gear. It is an early beta. [More about the mod](/projects/ancestral-ruins/).

## Ancient Obsession

A colonist gets stuck on an ancient wreck and asks for parts. Bring them in time, and the wreck becomes a named legendary machine. Ancient Restoration 0.6 has the same thing built in. [More about the mod](/projects/ancient-obsession/).
