Version 0.6.2 of Ancient Restoration came out on the Steam Workshop on 8 November 2026. It is a small fix release, and old saves keep working.

- The Workshop page is now in Ukrainian, and the descriptions of all restored buildings are translated into Ukrainian.
- The mod is more reliable on RimWorld 1.7: when it hooks into the game, it now names its own code. Before, the game could quietly skip such a hook.
- Legends from the built-in Ancient Obsession are protected from breakdowns, and this is now checked in the game.

![The blueprint from the Workshop header: an ancient turret from wreck to modern.](assets/ancient-restoration/00-header.jpg)

What came in 0.6.0 and 0.6.1, including the built-in Ancient Obsession, is in [the post about 0.6.1](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-6-1/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
