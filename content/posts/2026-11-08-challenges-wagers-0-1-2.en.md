Version 0.1.2 of Challenges & Wagers came out on 8 November 2026. Nothing changes in play: the mod now has an icon in the game's mod list, a gold trophy cup and the storyteller's die, in the shared style of EGYS1N's mods.

![The mod's icon: a gold trophy cup and the storyteller's die on squared paper.](assets/challenges-wagers/mod-icon.png)

What the mod does and what came in 0.1.1 is in [the post about 0.1.1](/blog/challenges-wagers-0-1-1/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
