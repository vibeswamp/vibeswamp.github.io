Version 0.3.1 of Trodden Trails came out on 12 November 2026. It is a small update, and nothing changes in play:

- the mod now has its own icon in the game's mod list;
- the in-game mod description now matches the new Workshop page.

![The mod's icon: a path trodden to a house, on squared paper.](assets/trodden-trails/mod-icon.png)

What the mod does is in [the post about its release](/blog/trodden-trails-is-out/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
