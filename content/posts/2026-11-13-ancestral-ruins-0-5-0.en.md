Version 0.5.0 of Ancestral Ruins came out on 13 November 2026, as promised in [the post about 0.4.0](/blog/ancestral-ruins-0-4-0/). It is still a beta.

- The "Traces of the past" window at the start has a third row, "Who lives there now": nobody, descendants, or let fate decide.
- A small, poor settlement of the past colony's heirs may live near the ruins, with its colour and ideology. The stronger the colony was (wealth, people, years, defences), the likelier the descendants. A quick, simple colony leaves only ruins and graves.
- On high difficulty there are fewer descendants and they are angrier; a peaceful storyteller makes them kinder.
- Descendants react to how you treat their past. Opened graves and dismantled buildings anger them, burials and repairs please them. Each time a letter arrives.
- No free reactor: expensive and space tech such as reactors and AI cores lies in the ruins only as wrecks.
- The settings have a limit on descendant factions. Saves are compatible.
