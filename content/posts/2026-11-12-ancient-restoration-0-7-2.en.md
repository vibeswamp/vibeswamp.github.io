Version 0.7.2 of Ancient Restoration came out on 12 November 2026, again from players' comments.

- A new setting: you can allow a restoration even when it costs more than building the thing new. It is off by default.
- Why: with mods that make workbenches cheaper (mini workstations), the Restore button used to switch itself off. A player with an ice-age colony asked for it.
- The mod is updated for the new RimWorld 1.7 build.

What came last time is in [the post about 0.7.1](/blog/ancient-restoration-0-7-1/). If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
