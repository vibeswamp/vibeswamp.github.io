Version 0.9.0 of Seamless World came out on 8 November 2026. It is still a beta.

- Fog over the neighbors, optional: you pick it on a new welcome page. You see a neighbor zone only near your own people: a colonist, colony mech or vehicle sees 26 cells. Maps where you live never get fog.
- A new 2×2 watchtower in wood or metal (Misc tab) sees 40 cells, also across the edge. It needs no power, and nobody has to stand on it.
- Where you have been stays on your map under a light haze. Strangers there show only while someone is watching.
- New neighbor zones can be 100, 75 or 50 % deep. The zone keeps the size of your map, so borders line up, and beyond that depth there is a rock ridge the game spends no time on.
- "Prepare to remove the mod" in the settings: after it the save loads cleanly without Seamless World.
- As Above, So Below 2: on a colony with levels, the neighbors line up with the surface and the zones stay flat. Thanks to H3LL5HYDR4L15K and veoba for asking. Second Floor works alongside.
- A Ukrainian translation, and a small open API for other mods: [Trodden Trails](/projects/trodden-trails/) already uses it.
- Fixes: the fog no longer throws errors after loading a save, and on odd map sizes (225, 275, 325) the neighbors line up exactly.

![The mod as a blueprint: your map, the neighbors around it and a path across the edge.](assets/seamless-world/00-keyart.jpg)

The fog was promised in [the post about 0.8.0](/blog/seamless-world-0-8-0/), and now it is here. If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
