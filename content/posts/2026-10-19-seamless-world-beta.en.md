The early beta of Seamless / Borderless World appeared on the Steam Workshop on 19 October 2026. It is a mod for RimWorld 1.7.

With it, the edge of the map is no longer the end of the world. The six neighbouring tiles become real maps around your colony. Colonists cross the border alone or as a group, and you can call someone back by clicking their portrait. Zones where none of your people are cost almost nothing.

![Crossing as a group: colonists wait for each other at the border and step over together.](assets/seamless-world/04.jpg)

It is a beta, so save often and keep backups. The mod is [on the Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823099600), and [the mod page](/projects/seamless-world/) here has all the pictures.
