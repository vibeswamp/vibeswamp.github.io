Version 0.4.0 of Ancestral Ruins came out on 12 November 2026, the third update of the day after [0.2.0](/blog/ancestral-ruins-0-2-0/) and [0.3.0](/blog/ancestral-ruins-0-3-0/). It is still a beta.

- A "Traces of the past" window after you pick a landing site: how many past colonies to bring back (none, one, three or all) and which ones, those that suit this land or any. Under the buttons you see how many colonies fit. The window is turned on in the settings with "Ask at start". With it off, the mod brings back one colony with similar land by itself.
- Ruins settle on land like the one where the colony stood: the same or a close biome, similar temperature, rainfall and hills, river and coast. A desert colony will not end up near the pole. The place is searched 5–25 tiles from the new colony, and up to 50 if nothing similar is close.
- If the new world has the same seed and settings, the colony takes its old tile when that is within reach.
- Biomes from removed mods are compared by climate. Old records are read as before.
- Saves are compatible. In games already started, ruins are not moved.

![The ruins of an old colony in a new world, its solar panels still standing.](assets/ancestral-ruins/own-ruin.jpg)

Next comes 0.5.0, "Descendants": a strong past colony may leave living heirs. They are a faction of their own with the old ideology, and they care how you treat the ruins and graves.

If you are subscribed, Steam downloads the update by itself.
