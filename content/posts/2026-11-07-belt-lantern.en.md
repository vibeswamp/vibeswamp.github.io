Not every mod of mine is for RimWorld. Belt Lantern, a mod for the co-op submarine game Darkwater, came out on Thunderstore on 3 April 2026. All four versions, from 1.0.0 to 1.2.1, went up that same day.

The mod adds a belt slot for the gas lantern, so both hands stay free. V moves the lantern between your hand and the belt, L turns it on and off. The light shines all around you with a slight flicker of the flame. Other players see it too, and if you die, the lantern drops so your teammates can pick it up.

On 7 November 2026 it has 820 downloads. Among the mods for Darkwater on Thunderstore, only MorePlayers has more.

The mod is [on Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/darkwater/p/EGYS1N/Belt_Lantern/) and needs BepInExPack. More on [the mod page](/projects/belt-lantern/).
